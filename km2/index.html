Problem description

Twenty-one adults aged 70 or older with newly diagnosed acute myeloid leukemia (AML), judged not fit for intensive chemotherapy, started lower-intensity treatment. Patients were recruited over several months and the data were analysed at a fixed date, so follow-up ranges up to 35 weeks. The outcome is death from any cause. Patients still alive at the analysis date, or lost to follow-up, are censored at their last contact. The following survival times, in weeks from the start of treatment, were recorded:

Survival times (21 patients): 6, 6, 6, 7, 10, 13, 16, 22, 23, 6+, 9+, 10+, 11+, 17+, 19+, 20+, 25+, 32+, 32+, 34+, 35+

“+” denotes a censored observation: the patient was alive when last seen. This is an illustrative example; the times are adapted from the remission times of the 6-mercaptopurine group in Freireich et al. (1963).
The recorded week of a death or censoring, measured from the start of follow-up.tᵢ = observed follow-up time (weeks)
Patients still alive and under observation just before this week. Include patients who die or are censored at this week.nᵢ = number with follow-up ≥ tᵢ
Deaths at this week. Tied deaths are counted together; censored observations are excluded.dᵢ = number of deaths at tᵢ
Patients whose observation ends at this week without an observed death. They leave the next risk set.cᵢ = number censored at tᵢ
n after tᵢ = nᵢ − dᵢ − cᵢ
Conditional probability of surviving this week, given being alive and at risk just before it. With no deaths, it equals 1.pᵢ = 1 − dᵢ/nᵢ = (nᵢ − dᵢ)/nᵢ
Cumulative probability of surviving beyond this week. Multiply the conditional probabilities through this time.Ŝ(tᵢ) = Ŝ(tᵢ⁻) × (1 − dᵢ/nᵢ)
Ŝ(t) = ∏tᵢ ≤ t (1 − dᵢ/nᵢ)
